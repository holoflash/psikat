# psikat

A browser based tracker-like sequencer DAW.
Developed without **any** AI assistance for recreational and learning purposes.

## Why no AI?

Because I already made **psikat** and it's almost exactly what I envisioned.
[https://psikat.com](https://psikat.com/)

Almost...
It's certainly no "vibe slop" project. I've put in a at least few months of manual human hours into it.
But still, it's almost "done" and I'm not feeling the satisfaction that I should.

As the project progressed, the ideas kept coming, and during moments of the most excitement - self-discipline failed me.
"I know how this works, it's fine, I'll take a closer look later" - I said to myself, as I let the Agent figure out why a button looks weird after the latest **egui** update, or what the formula is for writing a biquad filter.

Don't get me wrong, agentic AI was very useful to get me to this point. I quickly iterated through ALOOOT of ideas that didn't pan out, tried a number of different stacks and was able to build **almost** exactly what I wanted to.

In the end, I learned a lot about **psikat** but not about everything else I should've learned.

This time, I want to learn!.<br>
I want to struggle and overcome.<br>
I want to do the best I can and let it suck if that's the best I got. <br>
I want to put my name on it and say - **"I did this!"**, without any disclaimers. <br>
